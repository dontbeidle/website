Kaalin is a toolkit for working with the Karakalpak language. Available on three platforms: Python, JavaScript, and CLI.

## Features

- **Script conversion** — bidirectional Latin ↔ Cyrillic transliteration
- **Number to words** — convert numbers up to 10³⁰ into Karakalpak words
- **Syllabification** — split words into syllables (Latin and Cyrillic)
- **String utilities** — Karakalpak-aware `upper()` / `lower()` that correctly handle special characters
