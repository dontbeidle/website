## About DontBeIdle

DontBeIdle is a volunteer developer community that builds open-source projects. We work together on open-source software and share our knowledge with others.

## Our Mission

To develop a culture of software engineering in Karakalpakstan and beyond. Everyone can contribute, regardless of their experience level.

## Join Us!

If you're interested in participating in open-source projects, join us. You can reach out via GitHub and Telegram.
